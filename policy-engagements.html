Multilateral Trade Inputs & Framework Engagements

The Global Trade & Services Committee actively contributes high-level strategic inputs on bilateral and multilateral agreements to facilitate seamless export of professional services and cross-border accounting mobility. Ongoing recommendations directly impact key frameworks across multiple global jurisdictions.

FTAs (Free Trade Agreements) CEPA (Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements) JTC (Joint Trade Committees) MRA / MoU (Mutual Recognition Agreements) EPTA (Early Progress Trade Agreements) WGTI (Working Groups on Trade & Investment) TMM (Trade Ministerial Meetings)

Chintan Shivir Collaborations & Ministerial Engagement

Reinforcing active domestic advocacy, the Committee partners closely with the Ministry of Commerce & Industry and the Services Export Promotion Council (SEPC) to spearhead focused advisory conventions like the institutional Chintan Shivir series. These integrated consultations synthesize actionable guidelines for expanding standard export practices and navigating international trade compliance.

ITEC Training Programmes under Ministry of External Affairs

Serving as a premier knowledge export hub, the Committee executes multi-jurisdictional executive programs under the prestigious Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation (ITEC) mechanism. Directed under the oversight of the Ministry of External Affairs, these initiatives share India’s state-of-the-art financial oversight frameworks, transparent digital tracking pipelines, and professional standards with delegate nations globally.
