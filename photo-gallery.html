Seminar on Global Opportunities for Indian Chartered Accountants

The Global Trade & Services Committee of ICAI, hosted by EIRC at ICAI Centre of Excellence, Kolkata, organised a full-day seminar focused on global opportunities for Indian Chartered Accountants. The programme featured insightful technical sessions on US taxation, global outsourcing, Australian accounting & SMSF, and technology enablement for international outsourcing. The event concluded with an engaging panel discussion on positioning Indian CAs as global professionals, bringing together industry experts and leaders to share valuable global insights and opportunities.
