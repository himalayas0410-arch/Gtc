ICAI – Invest India Strategic Partnership

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), through its Global Trade & Services Committee, has established a strategic partnership with Invest India following the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 10 February 2023. The partnership is valid for a period of five years, up to 9 February 2028.
This collaboration aims to strengthen India’s trade and investment ecosystem by facilitating policy dialogue, promoting cross-border investments, and supporting global economic engagement.

Key Areas of Engagement

Trade & Investment Facilitation
  • Participation in Trade Officers’ Roundtable discussions focused on enhancing inbound and outbound investment flows.
Policy Advocacy & Government Representation
  • Contributions on key matters related to trade, investment, and Free Trade Agreements (FTAs).
Strategic Inputs & Thought Leadership
Inputs provided on:
  • BRICS Presidency 2026
  • OECD STRI (Services Trade Restrictiveness Index) database
  • UNCTAD draft documents
  • Services export roadmap
Support in Trade Negotiations
  • Engagement and knowledge support across multiple FTA negotiations, including EU, New Zealand, UK, Chile, and others.
