Borderless Profession: India–UK | Pathways for Global Chartered Accountants

The recently concluded India–UK Global Summit at the prestigious ICAEW Auditorium, London, marked an important step towards strengthening collaboration between two of the world’s most respected accounting ecosystems.
Organised by the Global Trade & Services Committee, ICAI in collaboration with ICAEW and hosted by the London (UK) Chapter of ICAI, the India–UK Global Summit in London brought together over 90 participants and coincided with the 25-member ICAI UK Delegation visit, marking a significant initiative towards strengthening India–UK professional collaboration and expanding global opportunities for Indian Chartered Accountants.
The summit focused on one defining theme — the emergence of a truly borderless profession, where Chartered Accountants are increasingly contributing across global markets through technology, mobility, and specialised expertise.
The programme commenced with an insightful inaugural session where distinguished dignitaries including Mr. David Franklin, Deputy CEO, ICAEW, and Ms. Nidhi Mani Tripathi, Hon’ble Minister (Economic), High Commission of India, London, shared perspectives on the growing India–UK partnership, global mobility, and the evolving role of Chartered Accountants.
A dedicated session on “Pathways for ICAEW” featured CA Mayuri Chordia and Mr. Doug Withington discussing international pathways and opportunities for Indian Chartered Accountants.

Sincere Appreciation
The Global Trade & Services Committee, ICAI also extends its sincere appreciation to Audit Corpus for their valuable sponsorship support towards the India–UK Global Summit, London. Their association and contribution significantly supported the initiative in fostering global professional collaboration, knowledge exchange, and international networking opportunities for Chartered Accountants.
The summit concluded with an engaging networking session fostering meaningful India–UK professional connections and collaborations.
A recurring theme across all discussions was clear — the profession is evolving from a jurisdiction-based model to a globally connected ecosystem driven by trust, technology, collaboration, and specialised expertise.
The India–UK Global Summit reflected the growing international presence of Indian Chartered Accountants and reinforced the commitment of the Global Trade & Services Committee, ICAI towards creating greater global opportunities, professional mobility, and international collaborations for the profession.
