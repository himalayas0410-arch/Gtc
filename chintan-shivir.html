Chintan Shivir 2025 on Professional Services with the Ministry of Commerce & Industry and SEPC

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), in collaboration with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and the Services Export Promotion Council (SEPC), organised a Chintan Shivir on Professionall Services titled "Expanding Global Horizons: Opportunities for Indian Professionals" on 23rd December 2025.

The event witnessed the participation of over 100 delegates from industry, the professional fraternity, and Ministries. Four focused sessions were conducted, covering key themes such as making professionals globally ready; strengthening international mobility through Mutual Recognition Agreements (MRAS) and Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs); developing global networks through the formation and expansion of professional chapters abroad; and leveraging Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) to boost exports of professional services.
