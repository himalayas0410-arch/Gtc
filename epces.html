Strategic Partnership

ICAI – Export Promotion Council for EOUs & SEZs (EPCES)

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), through its Global Trade & Services Committee, has established a strategic partnership with the Export Promotion Council for EOUs & SEZs (EPCES) following the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 25 October 2023. The partnership is valid for a period of five years, up to 24 October 2028.

Key Focus Areas of the MoU

Focus Areas
  • Promoting collaboration, innovation, and shared progress
  • Strengthening and refining financial reporting and auditing practices in EOUs & SEZs
  • Enhancing the overall business environment in India

Key Initiative

Accounting Process Outsourcing (APO) Portal
  • Facilitates and promotes outsourcing and export of accounting services
