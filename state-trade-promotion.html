Pillar 1: Trade & Market Integration State Collaboration Model

Involving State Government in Promoting Trade

Driving regional trade growth through government collaboration and policy alignment to build export competitiveness across Indian states.

Regulatory Optimization

Partnering with State Governments to streamline approvals, simplify compliance mandates, and build export-friendly environments for businesses.

Policy Advocacy

Advocating for state-level trade policies aligned with national export goals to ensure federal synergy and cohesive foreign trade actions.

Enterprise Support

Empowering local manufacturers, agricultural units, and service provider MSMEs to leverage emerging cross-border trade opportunities.

Infrastructure Growth

Fostering public-private partnerships to enhance logistics, storage facilities, testing laboratories, and transport corridors at state borders.

National Integration

Connecting state industrial efforts directly with central trade facilitators such as DGFT and specialized Export Promotion Councils.

FDI Attraction

Supporting the optimization of Special Economic Zones (SEZs) and state industrial corridors to attract Foreign Direct Investment (FDI).
