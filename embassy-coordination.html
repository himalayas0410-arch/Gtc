Pillar 2: Global Services & Professional Mobility Diplomatic Coordination

Coordination with Embassies & High Commissions

Expanding the global footprint of Indian CAs through structured diplomatic and institutional engagement.

Global Professional Placement

Liaising with Embassies in India and Indian High Commissions abroad to map international talent demands and facilitate direct professional placements for Indian Chartered Accountants.

Mobility & Cross-Border Recognition

Supporting global professional mobility, easing regulatory and visa protocols, and securing cross-border recognition of qualifications with key partner countries.

Diplomatic Footprint Expansion

Creating dedicated diplomatic channels to promote and expand the CA profession's brand presence and structural footprint on the global stage.

Bilateral Institutional Engagements

Organizing strategic briefings, networking roundtables, and diplomatic events to foster continuous knowledge sharing and mutually beneficial professional ties.
