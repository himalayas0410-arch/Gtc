ICAI–IFSCA Strategic Collaboration

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), through its Global Trade & Services Committee, has established a strategic partnership with the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) through an MoU signed on 24 November 2023, valid until 23 November 2026.
This collaboration focuses on promoting opportunities in international financial services, particularly at GIFT City, and strengthening India’s position as a global financial hub.
